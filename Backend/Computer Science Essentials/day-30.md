# Day 30: Capstone: CS Foundations Portfolio & Review

**Week 4 · Systems, Design & Practice**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Consolidate 30 days of learning into a reviewable portfolio
- Self-assess strengths and gaps honestly
- Set a concrete plan for continued growth

## 📚 Topics
- Portfolio assembly
- Honest self-assessment
- Planning a personalized next-30-days

## 💡 Core Ideas
- A portfolio isn't just proof for others — assembling it forces you to re-touch everything you built, which is itself powerful spaced repetition.
- The goal of a 30-day foundation isn't mastery, it's a solid, connected mental map you can deepen indefinitely — identifying gaps now is a feature, not a failure.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Assemble a single repo/portfolio linking to: your Week 1 data structures library, your Week 2 algorithm solutions, your Week 3 concept write-ups, and your Week 4 system design doc.
- [ ] Redo, from memory and without notes, ONE problem from each week (Big-O analysis, a sorting/searching problem, a DP problem, and the system design trade-off explanation) to test true retention.
- [ ] Write a `NEXT_STEPS.md`: 3 topics you want to go deeper on, and a rough plan (even informal) for the next 30 days — e.g., language specialization, a specific system design deep dive, or a formal DSA interview prep sprint.

## 🎤 Interview-Style Questions
Try answering these out loud, without notes, before checking any reference:

- Which single day of this course changed how you think about engineering the most, and why?
- What's the one concept you'd want to re-explain to a friend to prove you really understand it?
