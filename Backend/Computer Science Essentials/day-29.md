# Day 29: System Design Practice: Design a Real System

**Week 4 · Systems, Design & Practice**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Apply Week 4's concepts (databases, distributed systems, scaling, security) to a full design
- Practice structuring and communicating a design under time pressure
- Identify and defend trade-offs explicitly

## 📚 Topics
- End-to-end system design walkthrough
- Requirements gathering (functional & non-functional)
- Trade-off articulation (the most important system-design skill)

## 💡 Core Ideas
- A strong system design answer spends real time on requirements and constraints before jumping to boxes and arrows — most weak answers skip straight to 'architecture'.
- Every design decision is a trade-off; being able to say 'I chose X over Y because Z, and here's what that costs us' is more valuable than knowing any specific 'correct' architecture.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Pick ONE: design a URL shortener, a rate limiter, or a simple chat system. Spend 45–60 minutes designing it end-to-end: requirements, scale estimate, high-level architecture (diagram), data model, and 2–3 explicit trade-offs you made.
- [ ] Write your design as a `SYSTEM_DESIGN.md` — treat it like something you'd hand a teammate or interviewer.
- [ ] Identify the single biggest bottleneck in your design at 100x the scale you designed for, and write how you'd address it.

## 🎤 Interview-Style Questions
Try answering these out loud, without notes, before checking any reference:

- What was the hardest trade-off you had to make in your design, and why did you decide the way you did?
- Where does your design break first as load increases, and what's your first fix?
