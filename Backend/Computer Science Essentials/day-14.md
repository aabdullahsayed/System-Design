# Day 14: Week 2 Capstone: Algorithm Problem Set

**Week 2 · Algorithms**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Apply everything from Week 2 under time pressure
- Practice explaining your approach out loud (interview simulation)
- Build the habit of complexity analysis before coding

## 📚 Topics
- Pattern recognition across problem types
- Talking through an approach before coding (interview technique)
- Self-review: correctness, edge cases, complexity

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Most 'new' interview problems are recombinations of patterns you already know (two-pointer, sliding window, BFS/DFS, heap, backtracking) — spend energy on pattern recognition, not memorizing solutions.
- State your approach and its complexity *before* writing code — it catches bad approaches early and is exactly what real interviews expect.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Solve 6 problems, one from each Week 2 topic (recursion, sorting, binary search, trees, heaps, graphs), timing yourself at 25–35 minutes each.
- [ ] For each, write a one-paragraph explanation of your approach and its time/space complexity BEFORE looking at any solution.
- [ ] Review any you got wrong or timed out on — write down the pattern you missed.

## 🎤 Interview-Style Questions
Try answering these out loud, without notes, before checking any reference:

- Pick your weakest of the 6 topics — what specifically made it hard: the pattern, the implementation, or the edge cases?
