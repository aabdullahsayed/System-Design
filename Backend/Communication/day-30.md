# Day 30: Capstone: Communication Growth Plan

**Week 4 · Advanced & Professional Application**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Consolidate 30 days of practice into a clear self-assessment
- Identify which skills to keep deliberately practicing
- Build a concrete, sustainable plan for continued growth

## 📚 Topics
- Reviewing growth across the four weeks
- Turning a month of practice into a lasting habit
- Setting a realistic, specific ongoing practice plan

## 💡 Core Ideas
- A month of focused practice builds awareness and some new habits, but without a concrete continuation plan, most of it fades within a few weeks — this capstone exists specifically to prevent that.
- You don't need to keep working on all 29 previous topics — identifying the 2-3 that matter most for your specific goals and context is what makes ongoing practice sustainable.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Reread your Day 7 and Day 29 self-assessments. Write a half-page reflection on what's actually changed in how you communicate over the past 30 days.
- [ ] Pick the 3 skills from this course (out of listening, clarity, assertiveness, feedback, storytelling, public speaking, negotiation, etc.) that matter most for your goals right now.
- [ ] Write a `COMMUNICATION_GROWTH_PLAN.md`: your top 3 focus skills, one concrete weekly practice for each, and how you'll get ongoing feedback (a mentor, a peer, self-recording) over the next 90 days.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What's the single biggest shift in your communication over these 30 days — a skill, a habit, or just an awareness?
- Who's one person you trust to keep giving you honest feedback on this going forward, and have you actually asked them?
