# Day 5: Asking Good Questions

**Week 1 · Foundations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Distinguish open vs closed questions and use each intentionally
- Ask follow-up questions that deepen a conversation instead of redirecting it
- Avoid leading or loaded questions that bias the answer

## 📚 Topics
- Open vs closed questions
- Follow-up and clarifying questions
- Leading/loaded questions and how they bias responses
- The power of 'tell me more about that'

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Closed questions ('Did that work?') get you a fact; open questions ('What happened when you tried that?') get you a story — and stories carry far more useful information.
- The single highest-leverage follow-up in most conversations is simply 'tell me more about that' or 'what makes you say that?' — it costs nothing and almost always deepens the conversation.
- A leading question ('Don't you think X is a bad idea?') often gets you agreement, not truth — phrase questions neutrally if you actually want to know what someone thinks.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] In 3 conversations today, replace a closed question you'd normally ask with an open one, and notice the difference in the answer.
- [ ] Practice using 'tell me more about that' at least twice today when someone shares something interesting or concerning.
- [ ] Catch yourself asking a leading question at least once, and rephrase it neutrally in the moment.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What's one answer you got today that surprised you because you asked an open question instead of a closed one?
- Do you tend to ask questions to genuinely learn, or to confirm what you already believe? Be honest.
