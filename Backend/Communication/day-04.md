# Day 4: Nonverbal Communication & Body Language

**Week 1 · Foundations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Read and calibrate your own body language
- Notice nonverbal cues in others more accurately
- Align verbal and nonverbal signals for congruent communication

## 📚 Topics
- Posture, eye contact, gestures, facial expressions
- Tone of voice and pacing as a nonverbal channel
- Congruence: when words and body language agree (and when they don't)
- Cultural variation in nonverbal norms — a caution against overgeneralizing

## 💡 Core Ideas
- When verbal and nonverbal signals conflict, people almost always believe the nonverbal one — this is why 'I'm fine' said with crossed arms and a flat tone rarely lands as fine.
- Open posture, appropriate eye contact, and a relaxed pace generally read as confidence and openness — but norms vary by culture, so calibrate rather than apply rules universally.
- Your own body language shapes your internal state too, not just how others perceive you (e.g., posture can affect how confident you actually feel).

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Notice your own posture and gestures in your next 3 conversations. Are they congruent with your words?
- [ ] In one conversation today, deliberately watch the other person's body language for signs of engagement, discomfort, or disagreement — without saying anything about it.
- [ ] Practice a 2-minute self-introduction in a mirror or on video, paying attention to eye contact and posture.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What did you notice about your own nonverbal habits when you paid deliberate attention to them?
- Describe a moment today where someone's body language told you something their words didn't.
