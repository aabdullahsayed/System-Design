# Day 17: Vocal Delivery & Pacing

**Week 3 · Public Speaking & Presentations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Use pace, pitch, volume, and pause deliberately
- Eliminate filler words through practice, not just awareness
- Use strategic pauses for emphasis instead of rushing through them

## 📚 Topics
- Pace: why most speakers talk too fast under nerves
- Vocal variety: pitch and volume as emphasis tools
- The strategic pause
- Filler word elimination through recorded practice

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Nervous speakers almost universally speed up — deliberately slowing down 10-20% below your 'natural' pace, especially at key moments, reads as MORE confident, not less.
- A pause right before or after your most important point does more to signal 'this matters' than any word choice could — silence is an underused tool most speakers rush past out of discomfort.
- Filler words ('um', 'like', 'so') mostly fill the silence speakers are uncomfortable leaving — the fix isn't 'try not to say um', it's practicing tolerating the pause instead.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Record yourself giving a 90-second explanation of something. Count filler words, then re-record aiming for zero, allowing pauses instead.
- [ ] Practice the same 90 seconds again, this time deliberately pausing for 2 full seconds before your single most important sentence.
- [ ] Read a paragraph out loud at your normal pace, then again 20% slower. Notice how each version feels and sounds.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What's your personal filler-word tell, and in what situations does it get worse (nervousness, thinking on the spot, etc.)?
- How did it feel to sit in a 2-second pause instead of filling it? Did it feel longer to you than it probably sounded to a listener?
