# Day 2: Active Listening

**Week 1 · Foundations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Distinguish listening to respond from listening to understand
- Apply active listening techniques in real conversations
- Recognize your personal listening blocks (distraction, judgment, rehearsing)

## 📚 Topics
- Listening to respond vs listening to understand
- Reflective listening & paraphrasing
- Common listening blocks: judging, mind-reading, rehearsing, filtering
- The cost of interrupting

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Most people listen just long enough to formulate their reply, not long enough to actually understand — this single habit undermines more conversations than anything else.
- Paraphrasing back what you heard ('So what I'm hearing is...') does two things at once: verifies understanding AND makes the other person feel heard.
- Silence after someone finishes speaking is not a gap to fill — it's often where the most important part of what they meant is still forming.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] In your next 3 conversations, deliberately paraphrase what the other person said before responding with your own point.
- [ ] Practice waiting 2 full seconds after someone stops talking before you respond. Notice how it feels.
- [ ] Identify your #1 listening block (judging, rehearsing, distraction, filtering) by noticing it in action today.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- In today's conversations, how often were you actually listening vs. planning what to say next?
- What's one conversation today where paraphrasing changed the outcome or depth of the discussion?
