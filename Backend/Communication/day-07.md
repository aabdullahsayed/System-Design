# Day 7: Week 1 Capstone: Communication Self-Assessment

**Week 1 · Foundations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Consolidate the week's listening, clarity, nonverbal, and feedback skills
- Get honest external input on your communication habits
- Set specific goals for the rest of the 30 days

## 📚 Topics
- Self-assessment vs external assessment
- Turning insight into a concrete practice goal

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Self-perception of your own communication is notoriously unreliable — the goal of this capstone is to combine a week of self-observation with real external input.
- A vague goal like 'communicate better' won't change behavior — a specific one like 'pause 2 seconds before responding in meetings' will.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Write a half-page honest self-assessment: your communication style, your biggest listening block, and one nonverbal habit you noticed this week.
- [ ] Ask 2 people (one at work, one personal) for one specific piece of feedback on how you communicate. Use the SBI structure from Day 6 to ask for it clearly.
- [ ] Based on this week, write ONE specific, observable communication goal for the next three weeks (e.g., 'pause before responding', 'ask one open question per meeting').

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What was the biggest gap between how you see your communication and how others described it?
- What's the one habit from this week you most want to keep practicing?
