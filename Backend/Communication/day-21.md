# Day 21: Week 3 Capstone: Deliver a Talk

**Week 3 · Public Speaking & Presentations**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Combine structure, delivery, visuals, and Q&A handling into one complete talk
- Get real feedback on a real delivery, not just practice in isolation
- Practice the full cycle: prepare, deliver, receive feedback, reflect

## 📚 Topics
- Full-cycle talk preparation and delivery
- Soliciting and using real feedback on delivery

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Practicing pieces in isolation (structure one day, delivery another) is useful, but nothing substitutes for delivering one complete talk end-to-end and seeing how the pieces interact under real pressure.
- Feedback on a live delivery — from a real person, not just self-review — surfaces blind spots that solo practice never will.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Prepare a 3-5 minute talk on a topic you know well, using Day 16's narrative structure and, if useful, one supporting visual from Day 18.
- [ ] Deliver it live to at least one real person (in person or video call), applying pacing and pause techniques from Day 17.
- [ ] Ask for 2 specific pieces of feedback afterward (using SBI from Day 6) and take at least one live question, using the Q&A structure from Day 19.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What felt different about delivering live versus practicing alone?
- What's the single most useful piece of feedback you received, and what will you do differently next time?
