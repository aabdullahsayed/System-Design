# Day 14: Week 2 Capstone: Handle a Real Difficult Conversation

**Week 2 · Interpersonal & Workplace**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Apply assertiveness, emotional intelligence, and conflict-resolution skills to a real situation
- Prepare deliberately for a hard conversation instead of winging it
- Debrief honestly on what worked and what didn't

## 📚 Topics
- Preparing for a difficult conversation using this week's tools
- Debriefing a real conversation objectively

## 💡 Core Ideas
- Preparation is what separates a difficult conversation that stays productive from one that derails — walking in with a clear sense of your ask, the other person's likely interests, and your own emotional triggers changes the outcome dramatically.
- A good debrief separates 'what happened' from 'how I feel about what happened' — both are useful, but conflating them makes it hard to learn objectively.

## 🛠️ Practice Exercises
- [ ] Identify one real conversation you've been avoiding or dreading. Using Day 10's framework, write out the facts, feelings, and interests (yours and theirs) before you have it.
- [ ] Have the conversation this week, using an 'I' statement (Day 8) to open and active listening (Day 2) throughout.
- [ ] Debrief in writing afterward: what went well, what you'd do differently, and one thing you learned about the other person's perspective.

## 🪞 Reflection Prompts
Write these out, don't just think them:

- What surprised you most about how the conversation actually went, compared to how you imagined it?
- Which specific tool from this week (I-statements, pausing, naming interests) made the biggest difference?
